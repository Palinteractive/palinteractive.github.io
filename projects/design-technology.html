Tools built around architectural problems

I use AI and computational design as a way to make project specific workflows rather than as a generic visualization layer. These are prototypes and active development projects.

01 / Generative Test Fit Tool

A reinforcement learning / generative planning prototype that begins with predetermined unit layouts and rearranges them across a site according to explicit architectural rules including adjacency, orientation, view priorities, density, circulation and other project specific criteria.

03 / Open Source Rhino → Revit Bridge

A workflow in development that reads Rhino geometry together with BIM intent and translates it into native Revit elements or loadable families. The objective is not simply to move geometry; it is to preserve categories, materials, parameters, cut behavior, schedules and update tracking.
